UNIT IV: FINANCIAL STATEMENT PREPARATION AND ADJUSTMENTS

Practice Pack: Class Test | Quiz | Assignment | Case Studies
Suggested Total Practice: 100 marks

1. Important Concepts

\(\text{Gross Profit}=\text{Net Sales}-\text{Cost of Goods Sold}\)
\(\text{Net Profit}=\text{Total Revenue}-\text{Total Expenses}\)

Adjustments ensure that income and expenses are reported in the correct accounting period. The exact presentation of closing inventory and other adjustments depends on the accounting framework and the question's instructions.

5. Case Studies — 30 Marks

Case Study A: Year-End Adjustments (15 Marks)

At 31 March, a business has salaries paid of ₹36,000, with ₹4,000 outstanding. Insurance paid during the year is ₹12,000, of which ₹3,000 relates to the next accounting period. Equipment cost is ₹1,00,000, and depreciation for the year is ₹10,000.

  1. Calculate the salary expense for the year. [3]
  2. Calculate insurance expense for the year. [3]
  3. Calculate the equipment's carrying amount after depreciation, assuming no opening accumulated depreciation or disposal. [4]
  4. Explain the effect of each adjustment on profit and financial position. [5]

Case Study B: Inventory and Profit (15 Marks)

A retailer reports opening inventory ₹30,000, purchases ₹1,20,000, purchase returns ₹10,000, direct carriage inward ₹5,000, net sales ₹2,00,000 and closing inventory ₹35,000. Operating expenses are ₹25,000.

  1. Calculate net purchases. [3]
  2. Calculate cost of goods sold, including carriage inward. [4]
  3. Calculate gross profit. [4]
  4. Calculate net profit before tax, assuming no other income or expenses. [4]

6. Final Accounts Checklist

  • Record every year-end adjustment once in the appropriate accounts.
  • Check whether an adjustment affects profit, financial position or both.
  • Verify the balance sheet equation: Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
  • Show workings for inventory, depreciation and outstanding or prepaid items.
