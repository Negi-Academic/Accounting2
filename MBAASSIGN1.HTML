Assignment Questions
Question 1. Introduction to Financial Accounting (10 Marks)

Define financial accounting and explain its meaning, nature, objectives and importance in modern business organisations. Discuss how financial accounting helps in recording, classifying, summarising and communicating financial information to different stakeholders.

Question 2. Concepts, Conventions and Postulates of Accounting (10 Marks)

Explain the meaning and significance of accounting concepts, conventions and postulates. Discuss the major accounting principles, including the business entity concept, going concern concept, money measurement concept, dual aspect concept, consistency convention, prudence convention and full disclosure convention, with suitable examples.

Question 3. Accounting as a Language and Information System (10 Marks)

"Accounting is known as the language of business." Explain this statement in detail. Also discuss accounting as an information system and describe how financial information is collected, processed and communicated to support business decision-making.

Question 4. Users of Accounting Information (10 Marks)

Who are the different users of accounting information? Classify them into internal and external users. Explain the information requirements and interests of management, employees, investors, creditors, government authorities, customers and other stakeholders.

Question 5. Role of Accounting in Economic Development (10 Marks)

Discuss the role of accounting in the economic development of a country. Explain how accounting contributes to efficient resource allocation, investment decisions, financial transparency, accountability, taxation, business growth and overall economic stability.
