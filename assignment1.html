Assignment - Unit I

Topic: Introduction to Financial Accounting Framework

Part A: Theory Questions

  1. Define accounting and explain its objectives.
  2. Differentiate between accounting concepts and accounting conventions.
  3. Explain the money measurement, business entity and going concern concepts.
  4. Discuss the users of accounting information and their information needs.
  5. Explain how accounting supports economic development.

Part B: Application-Based Questions

  1. A business owner uses business cash to pay personal household expenses. Identify the accounting concept involved and explain its treatment.
  2. A company has followed the same depreciation method for several years. Identify the relevant convention and explain its importance.
  3. Explain why a bank, an investor and a government department may need different accounting information from the same company.

Submission Guidelines

Write answers in your own words. Use examples, suitable headings and a brief conclusion. Suggested length: 5-7 pages.
