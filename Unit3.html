4. Case Studies — 30 Marks

Case Study A: Trial Balance Difference (15 Marks)

A business prepares a trial balance. The debit total is ₹95,000 and the credit total is ₹91,000. On investigation, it finds that a cash payment of ₹4,000 for rent was entered in the cash account but was not posted to the rent account.

  1. Explain why the trial balance differs. [4]
  2. Prepare the correcting journal entry, assuming the cash account has already been credited correctly. [4]
  3. Will the correction alone always resolve every possible difference? Explain. [3]
  4. Suggest two procedures to reduce posting errors. [4]

Case Study B: Digital Accounting System (15 Marks)

A small retailer moves from handwritten books to accounting software. Staff members share one login, backups are not taken regularly, and invoices are sometimes entered twice.

  1. Identify three risks in the current process. [5]
  2. Recommend suitable controls for login access and backups. [4]
  3. Explain how duplicate-entry checks and bank reconciliation could help. [3]
  4. Suggest two reports the owner should review regularly. [3]
