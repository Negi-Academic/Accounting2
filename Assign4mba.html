Assignment Questions
Question 1. Meaning and Objectives of Financial Statements (10 Marks)

Define financial statements and explain their meaning, objectives and importance. Discuss how financial statements provide information about the financial position, financial performance and cash flows of a business organisation.

Question 2. Preparation of Trading Account and Profit and Loss Account (10 Marks)

Explain the meaning, objectives and importance of Trading Account and Profit and Loss Account. Discuss the items recorded in both accounts and explain the procedure for calculating Gross Profit and Net Profit with suitable examples.

Question 3. Preparation of Balance Sheet (10 Marks)

What is a Balance Sheet? Explain its meaning, features and importance. Discuss the classification of assets and liabilities and explain the procedure for preparing a Balance Sheet with a suitable format.

Question 4. Adjustments in Financial Statements (10 Marks)

Explain the meaning and importance of adjustments in the preparation of financial statements. Discuss the accounting treatment of outstanding expenses, prepaid expenses, accrued income, income received in advance and depreciation with suitable examples.

Question 5. Financial Statements with Adjustments (10 Marks)

Explain the procedure for preparing final accounts with adjustments. Discuss the treatment of closing stock, bad debts, provision for doubtful debts, depreciation and interest on capital in the Trading Account, Profit and Loss Account and Balance Sheet.
