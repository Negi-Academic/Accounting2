Assignment Questions
Question 1. The Accounting Cycle (10 Marks)

Define the accounting cycle and explain its meaning, objectives and importance. Discuss the various steps involved in the accounting cycle, starting from the identification of financial transactions to the preparation of financial statements.

Question 2. Journal and Its Sub-divisions (10 Marks)

What is a Journal? Explain its meaning, features and importance in accounting. Discuss the different types of special journals or subsidiary books, including Purchase Book, Sales Book, Purchase Returns Book, Sales Returns Book, Cash Book and Bills Receivable and Payable Books.

Question 3. Ledger and Trial Balance (10 Marks)

Explain the meaning, objectives and importance of a Ledger. Discuss the process of posting entries from the Journal to the Ledger. Also explain the meaning, objectives, preparation and limitations of a Trial Balance with suitable examples.

Question 4. Computerized Accounting Systems (10 Marks)

Define computerized accounting and explain its features, objectives and importance. Discuss the advantages and limitations of computerized accounting systems in comparison with traditional manual accounting systems.

Question 5. Computerized Accounting and Its Implications (10 Marks)

Discuss the major implications of computerized accounting in modern business organisations. Explain how accounting software improves accuracy, speed, data storage, reporting, financial control and decision-making. Also discuss the importance of data security and backup systems.
