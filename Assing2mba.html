Assignment Questions
Question 1. Accounting Systems and Transaction Processing (10 Marks)

Define an accounting system and explain its meaning, objectives and importance. Discuss the various stages involved in transaction processing and explain how an accounting system helps in maintaining accurate financial records.

Question 2. Accounting Equation (10 Marks)

What is the accounting equation? Explain its meaning, importance and basic components. Discuss the relationship between Assets, Liabilities and Owner's Equity with suitable examples. Also explain why the accounting equation always remains balanced.

Question 3. Transactions and Their Effects on Accounting Equation (10 Marks)

Explain the meaning of business transactions and discuss their effects on the accounting equation. Illustrate how different transactions, such as capital introduced, purchase of goods, payment of expenses, credit purchases and cash sales, affect assets, liabilities and owner's equity.

Question 4. Classification of Accounts (10 Marks)

Explain the different types of accounts and their classification under the traditional approach. Discuss Personal Accounts, Real Accounts and Nominal Accounts along with their rules of debit and credit. Give suitable examples of each type of account.

Question 5. Owner's Equity, Revenues and Expenses (10 Marks)

Explain the concepts of Owner's Equity, Revenues and Expenses. Discuss their relationship with the accounting equation and explain how revenue and expense transactions influence the financial position and profitability of a business.
