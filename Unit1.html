2. Quiz — 20 Marks

Choose the correct answer. Each question carries 2 marks.

  1. Which concept separates the owner from the business?
    (a) Matching (b) Business entity (c) Conservatism (d) Materiality
  2. The assumption that a business will continue operating is:
    (a) Going concern (b) Dual aspect (c) Realisation (d) Consistency
  3. Which is an external user of accounting information?
    (a) Storekeeper (b) Production supervisor (c) Investor (d) Cashier
  4. Recording transactions in monetary terms follows the:
    (a) Money measurement concept (b) Matching concept (c) Entity concept (d) Materiality concept
  5. Matching expenses with related revenue is called:
    (a) Dual aspect (b) Matching (c) Consistency (d) Disclosure
  6. Which statement is correct?
    (a) Accounting records only cash transactions
    (b) Accounting communicates financial information
    (c) Accounting ignores business decisions
    (d) Accounting is only for tax calculation
  7. Comparability is improved by applying methods consistently. (True/False)
  8. Employees may use accounting information to assess job security. (True/False)
  9. Financial accounting supports lending decisions. (True/False)
  10. Accounting information should be relevant and reliable. (True/False)

4. Case Studies — 30 Marks

Case Study A: Owner and Business (15 Marks)

Riya runs a stationery shop. She pays ₹12,000 of her personal home rent from the shop's bank account. She records the payment as a business rent expense. She also changes the inventory valuation method every year without a valid reason.

  1. Which accounting concept is affected by the personal payment? [3]
  2. Explain the correct accounting treatment of the personal payment. [4]
  3. Which convention is affected by frequent changes in valuation methods? [4]
  4. Why is consistency important for users of financial statements? [4]

Case Study B: Accounting Information and Decisions (15 Marks)

A bank receives financial statements from two businesses seeking loans. Business A provides complete records and explains its accounting policies. Business B provides incomplete records and omits important liabilities.

  1. Which business gives the bank more useful information, and why? [4]
  2. Identify two users of the financial statements and their information needs. [4]
  3. Explain why full disclosure matters. [3]
  4. Describe two ways reliable accounting information can support economic development. [4]
