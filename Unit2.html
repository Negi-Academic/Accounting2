UNIT II: ACCOUNTING SYSTEMS AND TRANSACTION PROCESSING

Practice Pack: Class Test | Quiz | Assignment | Case Studies
Suggested Total Practice: 100 marks

1. Key Accounting Equation

\(\text{Assets}=\text{Liabilities}+\text{Owner's Equity}\)

Every transaction must preserve the accounting equation. Revenue generally increases equity, while expenses and drawings reduce equity.

5. Case Studies — 30 Marks

Case Study A: Starting a Business (15 Marks)

Aman starts a repair shop with ₹1,50,000 cash. He buys tools for ₹30,000 cash, borrows ₹40,000 from a bank, earns service revenue of ₹12,000 in cash and pays wages of ₹4,000.

  1. Calculate total assets immediately after the initial investment. [3]
  2. Show the effect of purchasing tools. [3]
  3. Calculate closing cash and total assets after all listed transactions. Assume no other transactions. [5]
  4. Calculate closing equity using the accounting equation. [4]

Case Study B: Credit Transactions (15 Marks)

Meera Traders has cash ₹50,000, inventory ₹25,000 and equipment ₹40,000. It owes suppliers ₹15,000. During the month, it earns revenue of ₹18,000, incurs expenses of ₹6,000 and the owner withdraws ₹3,000 cash.

  1. Calculate opening owner's equity. [4]
  2. Calculate closing equity after revenue, expenses and drawings. [4]
  3. Explain why a credit purchase increases assets and liabilities. [3]
  4. State two ways the accounting equation helps detect errors. [4]
