Assignment Questions
Question 1. Tools of Financial Statement Analysis (10 Marks)

Define financial statement analysis and explain its meaning, objectives and importance. Discuss the various tools and techniques used for analysing financial statements. Explain how financial statement analysis helps investors, creditors and management in making business decisions.

Question 2. Horizontal Analysis, Vertical Analysis and Trend Analysis (10 Marks)

Explain the meaning and importance of Horizontal Analysis, Vertical Analysis and Trend Analysis. Discuss their methods of calculation, differences and applications in evaluating the financial performance and position of a business organisation with suitable examples.

Question 3. Ratio Analysis (10 Marks)

What is Ratio Analysis? Explain its meaning, objectives and importance. Discuss the major categories of accounting ratios, including Liquidity Ratios, Solvency Ratios, Activity Ratios and Profitability Ratios. Explain their important formulas and uses in financial decision-making.

Question 4. Cash Flow Analysis (10 Marks)

Define Cash Flow Analysis and explain its significance in financial management. Discuss the classification of cash flows into Operating Activities, Investing Activities and Financing Activities. Explain the preparation and importance of a Cash Flow Statement with suitable examples.

Question 5. Accounting Standard AS-3 (Revised) (10 Marks)

Explain Accounting Standard AS-3 (Revised) relating to Cash Flow Statements. Discuss its objectives, scope and major provisions. Explain the classification of cash flows under operating, investing and financing activities and discuss the methods used for reporting cash flows from operating activities.
